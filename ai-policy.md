This is an example of an in-class exercise I did to build a collaborative AI policy for students and their peers. I first had them fill out the AI policy document themselves, then had them get into small groups to discuss and form a general class policy and facilitated a whole-class discussion.

This was for FRST110, a first-year writing seminar with a writing-intensive component. Feel free to adapt and use for your own use cases. Please credit and link to this page if you do so.
